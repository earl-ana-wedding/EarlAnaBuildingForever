Wedding Venue Guide

Upon Arrival

When you arrive at the resort, a resort guard will assist you. Please let them know that you are a wedding guest.

Getting to the Wedding Venue

Follow the yellow arrows on the map to reach the wedding venue.

Parking spaces are available near the venue for your convenience.

Arriving Early?

If you arrive before the wedding starts, you may visit El Rio Café, where food, coffee, and refreshments are available for purchase while you wait.

Please note that El Rio Café is not part of the wedding reception, and any food or drinks purchased there will be at your own expense.

Please follow the red route on the map to get to the café.

Photoman

Our Photoman will be roaming around starting after the ceremony and throughout the reception program.

Don’t miss the chance to have your photo taken! If you spot the Photoman, feel free to approach and ask for a photo.

Reminder

This is an RSVP-only event. Guests whose names are not on the confirmed guest list will not be allowed to enter.
